SKYFALL

The world is the timer.

Your island is breaking apart. Collect three sky crystals, cross the void and awaken the ancient core. Pink orbs add 5 seconds. Every new island you reach restarts the timer, and the island behind you is destroyed. A dragon hunts every island: collect string, a bow frame and arrows, craft a bow, and kill the Warden to break the cage around the core diamond.

ISLAND LOST

The world has swallowed you.

BLOCKS & BUILDING

You start with 15 wood. Mining a block gives you that block type.

The gap to the next island is about 8 blocks of empty sky: build a bridge across it, or glide.

Craft (press I): 3 dirt + 1 wood = 2 stone.

GOAL & CLOCK

Collect 3 sky diamonds, cross the void, and press E at the ancient core.

Every island has its own timer: 2:00 · 2:10 · 2:00. At 0 the island collapses with you on it. Warnings come at 30 s and 10 s.

Reach the next island: the timer restarts and the island behind you is destroyed (no way back).

Pink orbs: +5 seconds each (islands 1 and 2).

Falling off into the void = game over.

SKY DIAMONDS & THE CAGE

3 diamonds in total. Walk into a glowing diamond to take it. The counter shows how many you have.

The 3rd diamond, the core diamond on the last island, sits in an iron cage with a red lock. You cannot touch it until you hold 2 diamonds and the Warden is dead: then the cage shatters.

Take it, then press E at the core: you win.

DRAGONS

A dragon hunts every island: 5 fire attacks. A RED RING shows where each lands, 2 s ahead.

Stand in the ring: -10 s and you are thrown back.

To kill it: throw blocks F at the HEAD only. Hits needed: Tyrant 3 (island 1) · Overlord 4 (island 2) · Warden 5 (island 3).

Block hits body or tail: -10 s, it bounces. Hits nothing: -15 s.

A dead Tyrant or Overlord builds you a bridge to the next island. A dragon you don't kill follows you with full health.

THE BOW (needed for the Warden)

Blocks bounce off the Warden: only arrows G hurt it, in the head (they work on every dragon).

Walk into each coloured beam: string (island 1), bow frame (island 2), 12 arrows + table (island 3).

Stand within 4.5 blocks of the table, press E: bow crafted. The top-left gear panel shows what you hold.
